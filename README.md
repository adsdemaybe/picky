# Picky

A personal AI shopping-assistant robot for physical stores — DubHacks 2026, Visa "Reimagine Shopping" track.

Plan your trip by text or iMessage, log in to the robot in-store, and it drives an efficient route through the store —
picking every item on your list, detouring past things you'll likely want (and asking first), staying inside your
budget, and checking out only with your explicit approval.
